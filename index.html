About

Senior writer and editor with six years at Gradient Learning, an education nonprofit working in partnership with the Chan Zuckerberg Initiative, where I led editorial strategy and executive thought leadership and produced content reaching more than 20,000 educators. Before that: content development at Nike, senior communication strategist at WebMD Health Services, and 14 years as a reporter and bureau editor in Oregon journalism.

I build messaging frameworks and brand voice standards, and I specialize in editorial work — reporting, human-interest storytelling, interviewing researchers and practitioners, and translating complex research and ideas into clear, practical stories for educators and general readers. University of Oregon, BS Journalism.
